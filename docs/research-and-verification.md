# Research Sources and Verification Register

## Source hierarchy
1. Official SIH statement/portal.
2. Official AICTE handbook, circulars, approval letters, amendments.
3. Official UGC regulations/notifications.
4. Official DPDP Act text, Gazette-notified Rules, MeitY publications.
5. Official APAAR/ABC documentation.
6. Official DigiLocker/API Setu partner documentation and authorized sandbox contracts.
7. Official institution schemas and signed integration agreements.
8. Peer-reviewed research and technical standards.
9. Secondary explainers only as leads, not final authority for legal/regulatory claims.

For each source record title, issuing authority, URL, publication/effective date, exact clause/page, access date, supported requirement, and applicability.

## Claim discipline
Mark secondary-source-only claims `[VERIFY]`; distinguish inference from source statements; label prototype thresholds; label mock integrations; say “designed to support” rather than “compliant” without assessment; never call a match score proof of fraud.

## Research log template
Claim; source title/authority; official URL; date; clause/page; exact supported statement; applicability/exclusions; implementation implication; owner; status (`UNVERIFIED`, `VERIFIED`, `SUPERSEDED`); last checked.
